The Brand

[ Who we are ]

We sit where Pakistani textile mastery meets UK brand culture. Bespoke embroidery, hand-finishing and crafted detail — we see real craft, and we bring it within reach for brands that care about their product.

[ What we do ]

Every Safir Wear piece is assembled with attention and respect for the detail — by people who genuinely love the craft. Concept, design, sampling, production, branding, fulfilment. One partner, no handoffs.
