Our Values

What drives us.

🧵
Craft Over Convenience
We never take shortcuts. Every garment is built with intention — the right fabric, the right construction, the right finish. Pakistani textile heritage isn't a marketing line for us. It's in every stitch.
🔍
Simple, Not Simplified
Quality clothing shouldn't require a logistics degree. We handle the complexity — fabrics, sampling, production, branding, fulfilment — so our clients can focus on their vision and launch with confidence.
💡
Fewer, Better Garments
We believe in making things that last. Not chasing volume. Every piece we produce is made with respect for the craft, the people who make it, and the people who wear it.
Timeline

Our journey so far.

2020

The Beginning

Safir Wear is founded as a creative house built on Pakistani textile heritage, with a mission to bring end-to-end clothing solutions to creators and brands.

2021

Creator Capsules Launch

We begin partnering with fashion influencers and content creators to produce their own branded clothing lines — from concept to fulfilment.

2023

Merchandising Programme

We expand into university and community merchandising, building quality apparel programmes for organisations across the UK.

2025

Today

We now serve brands, creators, universities, and businesses — offering a full suite from clothing concepts and sampling through to branding, packaging and UK fulfilment.
