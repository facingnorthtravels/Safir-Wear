FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers.

What are your minimum order quantities? +
Our minimums vary by product type, but we typically start from as low as 50 units per style per colour. We work with brands at every stage — whether you are launching your first collection or scaling an established line.
How long does sampling take? +
Most samples are completed and shipped within 48 hours of tech pack approval. Complex garments with custom fabrics or specialist finishes may take a little longer, but we will always confirm the timeline upfront before we begin.
Can I provide my own designs and tech packs? +
Absolutely. If you have existing designs, tech packs, or artwork, we can work directly from those. If you need help developing them, our team can build full tech packs from a sketch, mood board, or even just a conversation about your vision.
What is the typical production turnaround time? +
Standard production runs take 3 to 5 weeks from sample approval, depending on the complexity and volume of the order. Rush options are available for time-sensitive launches. We provide a clear timeline before production starts so there are no surprises.
Do you handle shipping and fulfilment? +
Yes. We offer full UK-based fulfilment — warehousing, pick, pack, and dispatch — either to your location or direct to your end customers. We can also handle branded packaging and inserts so the unboxing experience matches your brand.
What quality control processes do you have? +
Every order goes through multi-stage QC — from fabric inspection and pre-production checks to in-line monitoring and final inspection before dispatch. We photograph and document each checkpoint, so you have full visibility and confidence in what ships.
